Your Right to Data Deletion

In compliance with applicable data protection regulations including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and platform-specific requirements from Meta, Google, X, and LinkedIn, you have the right to request the deletion of your personal data from Celeritech Orbit at any time.

What Data We Delete

When you request data deletion, we will permanently remove:

  • Account information: Your name, email address, and encrypted password
  • OAuth tokens: All access tokens and refresh tokens for connected social media accounts (Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, X, LinkedIn)
  • Connected account data: Profile names, connection dates, and token metadata
  • Generated content: Blog posts, scheduled social media posts, generated images and videos
  • Usage preferences: Theme settings, saved configurations, and local storage data
  • Analytics data: Post performance data and scheduling history

How to Request Data Deletion

You can delete your data using any of the following methods:

1

Disconnect Individual Accounts

To remove data for a specific social media platform, go to Post Scheduler → Accounts and click the "Connected" button on any platform to disconnect it. This immediately revokes and deletes the OAuth token for that platform.

2

Delete Your Entire Account

To delete all your data from Celeritech Orbit, use the deletion request form below. This will permanently remove your account and all associated data within 30 days.

3

Contact Us Directly

You can also email us at privacy@celeritech.biz with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and include the email address associated with your account.

Facebook / Instagram Data Deletion

If you logged into Celeritech Orbit using your Facebook or Instagram account, you can also remove your data by:

  1. Go to your Facebook App Settings
  2. Find "Celeritech Orbit" in your list of apps
  3. Click "Remove" to revoke access
  4. This will trigger an automatic data deletion request on our end

Alternatively, submit a request using the form above or email privacy@celeritech.biz.

Processing Timeline

  • OAuth token revocation: Immediate (within seconds)
  • Account data deletion: Within 30 days
  • Backup purge: Within 90 days
  • Confirmation email: Sent upon completion
