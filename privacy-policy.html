1. Introduction

Celeritech Orbit ("we", "our", "us") is a content creation and social media management platform operated by Celeritech. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and share your personal information when you use our services at celeritech.biz and related applications.

By using Celeritech Orbit, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Password (stored using bcrypt encryption)

2.2 Connected Social Media Accounts

When you connect third-party social media accounts (Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, X/Twitter, LinkedIn), we collect and store:

  • OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens
  • Your public profile name on the connected platform
  • The date and time of connection
  • Token expiration information

We do not collect or store your social media passwords. Authentication is handled entirely through the platform's official OAuth 2.0 flow.

2.3 Content You Create

We store content created through our platform, including:

  • Blog posts and articles you generate or edit
  • Scheduled social media posts
  • Generated images and videos
  • SEO metadata and keywords

2.4 Usage Data

We may collect anonymized usage data such as feature usage patterns, generation counts, and error logs to improve our services.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain our content creation services
  • Authenticate your identity and manage your account
  • Publish content to your connected social media accounts on your behalf
  • Schedule posts according to your preferences
  • Display analytics and performance data from your connected accounts
  • Improve and develop new features
  • Communicate service updates and important notices

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We share your data with third parties only as necessary to provide our services:

  • Meta Platforms (Facebook/Instagram): We use the Meta Graph API to publish posts and retrieve analytics on your behalf.
  • Google (YouTube): We use the YouTube Data API to upload videos and access channel analytics.
  • X Corp (Twitter/X): We use the X API v2 to post tweets and retrieve engagement data.
  • LinkedIn: We use the LinkedIn API to share posts on your professional profile.
  • AI Service Providers: We use Anthropic (Claude), Google (Gemini), and Perplexity APIs to generate content. Prompts and topic descriptions are sent to these services but are not stored by them beyond processing.
  • Vercel: Our infrastructure provider for hosting and serverless functions.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored using industry-standard security practices:

  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt (one-way encryption)
  • OAuth tokens are stored in encrypted Redis databases
  • All data transmission is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS
  • We use Vercel's enterprise-grade infrastructure

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data at any time through your account settings
  • Disconnect any connected social media account at any time
  • Delete your account and all associated data (see our Data Deletion Policy)
  • Export your content and data
  • Opt out of non-essential data collection

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account:

  • Your personal information is permanently deleted within 30 days
  • OAuth tokens are immediately revoked and deleted
  • Generated content is removed from our servers
  • Anonymized, aggregated analytics data may be retained

8. Cookies

We use essential cookies and local storage to maintain your session, remember your preferences, and provide core functionality. We do not use third-party tracking cookies.

9. Children's Privacy

Celeritech Orbit is not intended for users under 16 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email or in-app notification. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
